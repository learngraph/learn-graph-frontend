# Platform content brief review

Status: **proposed for owner approval**. This reviews the populated content structure; it does not approve final copy or unverified claims.

## Territory verdict

The four topics remain distinct and justified. They form four different kinds of answer:

1. **The model** — what LearnGraph structurally is.
2. **Personal paths** — what that structure changes for one person over time.
3. **Learning evidence** — how learning activity and demonstrated capability become inspectable within that structure.
4. **Sovereignty** — under which control conditions the structure and its evidence can be trusted.

They should not receive uniform article treatment. Platform needs one conceptual anchor, one human sequence, one mechanism explanation, and one compact trust/facts page.

## The model

### Distinct job

Define the connected model beneath LearnGraph. It must explain graph logic without turning into a product inventory or repeating audience-specific applications.

### Does it earn the topic?

**Yes.** It is the conceptual anchor for the entire territory. Without it, the graph risks remaining a visual brand device instead of an understandable product model.

### Recommended content form

- short editorial explanation;
- one clear model diagram;
- a selective, current product view;
- one restrained action into the app.

### Source strength and writing need

The project presentation already contains strong conceptual material. It needs editing and compression rather than invention. The entities, relationships, diagram, and product view require product-owner confirmation or creation.

### Boundary

Do not explain personal progression, evidence handling, every audience, or every use case here. Point outward when those questions arise.

## Personal paths

### Distinct job

Show the learner-facing consequence of the model: orientation from a real starting point toward a goal, with understandable gaps and next steps.

### Does it earn the topic?

**Yes, with a strict boundary.** It earns its place only by showing movement through time. If it merely says that connected knowledge is useful, it collapses back into The model.

### Recommended content form

- one concise editorial statement;
- a small annotated sequence or current product walkthrough;
- only enough prose to explain why the route changes.

### Source strength and writing need

The conceptual argument is already strong in `Beyond the Score`, `From Map to Movement`, and the individual-learning material. New writing should wait for confirmation of the current orientation, gap, next-step, and adaptation behaviour.

### Boundary

Do not promise automatic personalisation or improved outcomes. The subject is legible movement, not algorithmic magic.

## Learning evidence

### Distinct job

Explain the product mechanism through which work, artifacts, feedback, progress, and demonstrated capability relate to the learning structure.

### Does it earn the topic?

**Conceptually yes; operationally still gated.** The subject is important and distinct, but the public page will only earn itself when the current evidence model can be described precisely. This is the strongest pressure point found during migration.

### Recommended content form

- a terse mechanism explanation;
- an evidence-lifecycle or relationship diagram;
- verified contextual cases attached where they demonstrate a specific claim;
- measured product evidence later, kept separate from learning evidence.

### Source strength and writing need

The source material strongly supports the principle that demonstrated work should matter more than attendance or a single score. It does not yet establish the exact implemented evidence types, ownership, visibility, review, portability, or recognition process. Those facts must come from product owners before substantial drafting.

### Boundary

Never use this node as a cabinet for logos, testimonials, adoption figures, or generic proof that LearnGraph works.

## Sovereignty

### Distinct job

State the conditions of control: inspectability, deployment, data location, integration, portability, and credible exit.

### Does it earn the topic?

**Yes, but not as a long article.** It is a distinct trust condition for institutions, funders, and protected contexts. Its strength will come from specificity rather than volume.

### Recommended content form

- a short editorial principle;
- a compact set of verified technical facts;
- links to inspectable artifacts when they exist;
- a later relationship to Research / Open Source if that territory becomes publishable.

### Source strength and writing need

The values and requirements are well supported by the project materials. Nearly every operational claim remains owner-gated. This topic needs factual answers more than creative prose.

### Boundary

Do not convert containerization, internal APIs, repository visibility, or intended open-core direction into public self-hosting, interoperability, security, or licensing claims.

## Relationship review

### Keep in the content graph

- **The model enables Personal paths.** This expresses the movement from shared structure to individual orientation.
- **Personal paths become legible through Learning evidence.** This is meaningful, but its exact product behaviour remains verification-sensitive.
- **Sovereignty constrains deployment of The model and handling of Learning evidence.** These are real architectural conditions, even if the visualization never draws both edges.
- **Sovereignty depends on inspectable Open-core artifacts.** Keep as a conditional cross-territory relationship while Research / Open Source remains hidden.

### Keep parked, not publicly asserted

- ITECH and Bildungszentrum Optimum as support for Learning evidence, pending attributable case packs and permission.

### Visualization caution

The registry may retain all meaningful relationships. The public graph should expose only the subset that improves orientation. A stored relationship does not automatically deserve an animated line.

## Approval recommendation

Approve all four **content structures**, with two explicit gates:

1. Learning evidence remains structurally approved but operationally unpublishable until its current mechanism is confirmed.
2. Sovereignty remains structurally approved but fact-led; it should not be handed out as a conventional prose-writing assignment.

After approval, Sol can draft **The model** from existing material. Personal paths should receive a bounded draft only after a current product walkthrough is selected. Learning evidence and Sovereignty should wait for owner facts rather than being filled with persuasive language.
