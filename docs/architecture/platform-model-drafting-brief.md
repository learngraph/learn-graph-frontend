## The one job

Answer: **What is the connected model beneath LearnGraph, and why do relationships make learning more navigable?**

The reader should leave understanding that the graph is product logic rather than a visual metaphor. This topic is the conceptual anchor for Platform.

## Required shape

Produce one compact node article containing:

1. a sharp title;
2. a one- or two-sentence lead;
3. two to four short editorial paragraphs;
4. optional language for a model-diagram caption;
5. one restrained action into the app.

Target roughly **140–220 words**, excluding the optional caption and action. Every paragraph must advance the explanation.

## Argument to carry

- A catalogue lists things; a graph can show how they relate.
- Learning becomes easier to orient within when a person can see a position, a goal, relevant foundations, possible next steps, and why those steps belong.
- The shared structure can give learners orientation while giving educators, organisations, and other supporters common ground.
- The graph supports human judgment. It does not replace it or prescribe one universal route.

These are conceptual claims. Do not silently turn them into claims about complete current functionality or demonstrated outcomes.

## Hard boundaries

Do not:

- inventory every product feature or graph entity;
- explain Personal paths or Learning evidence in depth;
- list audience groups or use cases;
- claim measured learning outcomes;
- imply algorithmic personalisation or automatic prescription;
- claim that every stated entity and relationship already exists in the current release;
- introduce open-core, self-hosting, data, or deployment claims;
- use generic innovation, transformation, empowerment, or future-of-learning language.

## Tone and form

Editorial, modern, technical, and self-possessed. Clear enough to understand immediately; distinctive enough to remember. No magazine-cover performance, no manifesto voice, and no decorative abstraction.

Prefer precise nouns and verbs. Avoid explaining what LearnGraph is _not_ unless the contrast makes the model materially clearer.
