# Territory card: Collaborate

Status: **architecture approved 2026-08-31**. `Build the offer` remains a provisional public label pending content review. Copy, claims, artifacts, root splitting, and visualization remain subject to later review.

## Root verdict

**Retain Collaborate provisionally as one root with two explicit internal clusters.**

Visitor question: **How can we work with LearnGraph or the team to make a difficult system, product, or learning context actionable?**

The source material contains two legitimate but distinct families. Hiding the distinction inside `Work together` would recreate the legacy site's ambiguity.

## Cluster A: Transformation services

### Find the constraint

- **Current path:** `/collaborate/find-the-constraint`
- **Purpose:** Make one consequential workflow, its dependencies, ownership, constraints, and first viable intervention visible.
- **Likely form:** concise service explanation + diagnostic/output diagram + verified experience artifact
- **Source strength:** strong conceptual offer; weak public proof

### Reduce manual load

- **Current path:** `/collaborate/reduce-manual-load`
- **Purpose:** Explain how process, architecture, data, integration, governance, and adoption can reduce repetitive work without creating another opaque system.
- **Likely form:** service explanation + good-fit/bad-fit distinction + implementation pattern + case when verified
- **Source strength:** coherent offer; automation and outcome claims need verification

### Build the offer

- **Current path:** `/collaborate/build-the-offer`
- **Label status:** provisional; the purpose is approved, but the wording may not distinguish product development, service design, proposition work, and implementation clearly enough
- **Purpose:** Turn an insufficiently defined product or service idea into a coherent proposition, viable first version, technical foundation, and validation path.
- **Likely form:** service explanation + discovery-to-release sequence + verified build artifact
- **Source strength:** coherent offer; no publishable case currently established

Shared principles—operating reality, architecture through adoption, early governance, and leaving capability behind—belong in the cluster orientation. They do not require a fourth service article.

## Cluster B: LearnGraph applications and partnerships

### Educational and public-system pilots

- **Working path:** `/collaborate/pilots`
- **Purpose:** Explain how LearnGraph can be applied and evaluated with an educational, public, community, or regional partner before broader rollout.
- **Likely form:** pilot-pattern diagram + contextual application artifacts + verified cases
- **Source strength:** rich application logic; limited verified completed-pilot evidence
- **Applications:** vocational learning and labour-market systems remain examples, not permanent navigation categories

### Implementation partnerships

- **Working path:** `/collaborate/partnerships`
- **Purpose:** Clarify what educational organisations, foundations, community networks, facilitators, technical partners, and LearnGraph each contribute to context-specific work.
- **Likely form:** role/relationship diagram + readiness criteria + distinct collaboration pathways
- **Source strength:** strong proposed role logic; little completed partnership evidence

`Protected Learning Maps` belongs as a clearly labelled **proposed application artifact** attached to pilots, partnerships, About / learning access, Platform / sovereignty, and Personal paths. It is not a completed case or a permanent topic by default.

## Remove or redistribute

- `Work together` should not remain an undifferentiated topic.
- Its working principles move to cluster/territory orientation.
- Its partnership invitation moves to Implementation partnerships.
- Its sprint/pilot/product formats move to the relevant topics.
- Its CTA becomes a utility action.

- Bildungszentrum Optimum — case candidate currently available only through a secondary summary
- energy/critical-infrastructure experience — service-evidence candidate requiring provenance and permission
- European interoperability experience — background/evidence candidate requiring named artifacts
- Protected Learning Maps — proposed application, never achieved impact

## Root-split threshold

Keep both clusters under Collaborate only if visitors can immediately distinguish:

- **engage the team for transformation work**, and
- **apply or develop LearnGraph with us**.

If the approved topics, operating models, audiences, and evidence remain substantially unrelated after review, the root-status audit should test a split. Do not split merely to simplify visualization.

## Owner decisions required

1. Which transformation offers are active, productised, and deliverable now?
2. Where does each offer stop: diagnosis, architecture, implementation, operation, or maintenance?
3. Which client/team experience can be named and evidenced?
4. Which LearnGraph pilots or implementations have actually occurred?
5. Which partnership types are actively sought now: funding, implementation, research, content, technical, or community?
6. What is the status and intended public role of Protected Learning Maps?

## Approved architecture

Model Collaborate as two unequal clusters with five candidate topics. Retire `Work together` as a topic. Do not decide a fifth root until the owner-truth and root-status audits are complete.
