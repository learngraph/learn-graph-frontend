# Territory card: Platform

Status: **architecture approved 2026-08-31**. Copy, claims, artifacts, and visualization remain subject to later review.

## Root verdict

**Retain Platform as a root territory.** It is the strongest and most coherent current territory.

Visitor question: **What is LearnGraph, how does it make learning navigable, and under what conditions can people and institutions trust it?**

The territory does not currently need an internal cluster. Its topics are unequal in role: the model is foundational; personal paths, learning evidence, and sovereignty are consequences or conditions of that model.

## Candidate topics

### 1. The model

- **Canonical path:** `/platform/model`
- **Purpose:** Explain the connected structure beneath LearnGraph: knowledge, skills or competences, goals, resources, people, paths, and their relationships.
- **Must accomplish:** Make the graph understandable as product logic rather than visual metaphor.
- **Likely form:** concise conceptual article + diagram + selective product view
- **Strong sources:** `A Living Map for Learning`, `Inside LearnGraph`, the shared-competence material from vocational and labour-market contexts
- **Supporting artifacts:** ITECH may demonstrate shared structure if the case and claims are publishable
- **Main risk:** trying to explain every audience and use case inside the model article
- **Gap:** a precise, current account of the implemented data/product model

### 2. Personal paths

- **Canonical path:** `/platform/personal-paths`
- **Purpose:** Show what the model changes for a learner: orientation, visible foundations and gaps, relevant next steps, and paths that can adapt.
- **Must accomplish:** Translate graph structure into human movement without promising magical personalisation.
- **Likely form:** editorial explanation + small product sequence, demonstration, or annotated screen
- **Strong sources:** `Beyond the Score`, `From Map to Movement`, legacy individual-learning synthesis
- **Supporting artifacts:** Bildungszentrum Optimum may substantiate differentiated starting points and continuity; a current product walkthrough may demonstrate the interaction
- **Main risk:** generic claims about motivation, personalisation, or learning outcomes
- **Gap:** confirmation of what path adaptation and guidance the current release actually performs

### 3. Learning evidence

- **Canonical path:** `/platform/evidence`
- **Working label:** `Learning evidence` rather than the ambiguous `Evidence`
- **Purpose:** Explain how work, artifacts, feedback, progress, and demonstrated capability can attach to the learning structure and remain inspectable.
- **Must accomplish:** Distinguish evidence inside the product from evidence that LearnGraph itself works.
- **Likely form:** mechanism explanation + diagram + contextual case references
- **Strong sources:** `Beyond the Score`, vocational evidence material, labour-market gap logic
- **Supporting artifacts:** ITECH and Bildungszentrum Optimum if verified; future measured product evidence
- **Main risk:** implying portability, assessment validity, or implemented functionality that is not yet real
- **Gap:** exact current capability, evidence types, ownership, review process, and portability status

### 4. Sovereignty

- **Canonical path:** `/platform/sovereignty`
- **Purpose:** Explain the institutional and personal control conditions: inspectability, deployment choices, data location, integration, and credible exit options.
- **Must accomplish:** State only what is operationally true and separate product implications from open-source implementation detail.
- **Likely form:** terse editorial principle + verified technical facts + link to Research / Open Source artifacts
- **Strong sources:** legacy open-core/independence synthesis; protected-context requirements in the Afghan pilot proposal
- **Supporting artifacts:** repositories, deployment documentation, security/integration references when public
- **Main risk:** presenting roadmap, broad security language, or a proposed protected deployment as current capability
- **Gap:** owner truth status for open-core scope, self-hosting, data residency, APIs/integrations, and user data control

## Material that does not currently earn another Platform topic

- **Inside LearnGraph** is product substantiation distributed across the four topics, not necessarily a fifth article.
- **Knowledge that travels and returns** deepens the model, evidence, and interoperability relationship. It should not become a node unless those homes prove insufficient.
- Vocational education, labour-market systems, individuals, and enterprise learning are applications of the platform, not Platform navigation categories.
- “Evidence LearnGraph works” belongs in reusable cases and proof artifacts, not inside the learning-evidence definition.

## Meaningful relationships

- The model **enables** personal paths.
- Personal paths **become legible through** learning evidence.
- Sovereignty **constrains and protects** how the model and evidence are deployed.
- The model **depends on / is made portable by** interoperability.
- Sovereignty **is substantiated by** public open-core and deployment artifacts.
- Learning access **depends on** orientation and usable personal paths.
- Protected or context-specific applications **require** sovereignty and deliberate visibility choices.

These relations belong in the content model. The visualization may expose only a useful subset.

## Owner decisions required

1. Which graph/model entities and relationships exist in the current release?
2. What can personal paths already do, and what remains direction?
3. What kinds of learning evidence can currently be created, attached, reviewed, or carried elsewhere?
4. Which sovereignty claims are current capability, roadmap, principle, or not intended?
5. Are the ITECH and Bildungszentrum Optimum cases current, approved, and attributable?
6. Which existing product video or screens still represent the current release?

## Approved architecture

Retain all four topics, rename `Evidence` to `Learning evidence` in the public interface, and do not add a fifth topic yet. Approve purposes and truth status before any rewriting.
