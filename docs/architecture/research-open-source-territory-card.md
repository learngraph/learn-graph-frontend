# Territory card: Research / Open Source

Status: **architecture reserved 2026-08-31; not approved for launch visibility**. The root and topic identities remain in the content graph while product, licensing, research, and contribution owners resolve the publication gate.

## Root verdict

**Keep Research / Open Source as the working root name, but do not grant it final root status until its public artifacts are verified.**

Visitor question: **Which parts of LearnGraph's technical and research work can be inspected, challenged, reused, or contributed to?**

This territory must be artifact-led. Principles and intentions may introduce work; they cannot impersonate repositories, specifications, methods, findings, or community governance.

## Candidate topics

### Open core

- **Current path:** `/research/open-core`
- **Purpose:** Define precisely what is open, under which license, how it can be inspected or deployed, and what remains outside that boundary.
- **Likely form:** scope/status table + repository/release artifacts + documentation
- **Current support:** inspectable frontend checkout, README, tests, and contribution conventions; broader open-core claims remain unverified
- **Critical caution:** the present CC BY-NC 4.0 terms should not automatically be described as a conventional open-source software license

### Interoperability

- **Current path:** `/research/interoperability`
- **Purpose:** Explain and demonstrate how meaning, evidence, or learning structures can cross system and organizational boundaries.
- **Likely form:** semantic/technical boundary explanation + schema/specification + integration artifact
- **Current support:** strong design intent and experience claims; no verified public specification or implementation case in the reviewed corpus

### Research questions

- **Current path:** `/research/questions`
- **Purpose:** Publish a small, dated set of active questions, their owners, methods, status, and outputs.
- **Likely form:** research agenda or working notes, explicitly distinguished from findings
- **Current support:** coherent question material; no established public research programme, methods, papers, or findings

### Contribute

- **Current path:** `/research/contribute`
- **Purpose:** Explain concrete contribution routes, governance, licensing, maintainers, review, safety, and decision authority.
- **Likely form:** contribution gateway linked to real repositories, issues, documentation, standards, or research briefs
- **Current support:** a frontend PR workflow and general invitation; no demonstrated wider contribution community or governance model

## Natural grouping to test

If artifacts justify it, two lightweight clusters may emerge:

- **Open Source** — open core, contribution
- **Research / Infrastructure** — research questions, interoperability

Do not introduce these clusters merely to rescue the combined root label.

## Publication threshold

- Fewer honest nodes are preferable to four aspirational essays.
- A repository link is not proof that the whole product is open.
- Consortium participation is not interoperability evidence.
- Product principles are not research findings.
- A standard pull-request workflow is not an active contributor community.

If only Open core has a verified artifact at launch, this territory should visibly contain only that work or lose root status until more exists.

## Owner decisions required

1. What exactly constitutes the open core?
2. Which repositories are public, canonical, current, and connected to the deployed product?
3. Which licenses apply to software, content, contributions, and commercial use?
4. What self-hosting, API, export, standards, or integration capabilities exist now?
5. Which research questions are actively owned and investigated, through what method?
6. Are outside contributions currently accepted, governed, and supportable?

## Reserved architecture

Preserve the root name during architecture review, but require an artifact audit before approval. Keep the four candidate concepts only as a backlog; publish each one solely when its evidence threshold is met.
